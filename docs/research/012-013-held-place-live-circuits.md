# What the first specimens left out

## Brief recovered

The later conversation asked for research into the material, cultural, mechanical and perceptual character of the catalogue and manual exchange, then a transformation that could challenge the existing card/cable forms. “Je ne sais quoi” is a human judgment, not an acceptance checkbox or a synonym for decorative polish.

The supplied research was preserved in Postack at commit `66287f0b0f1054c9ea5b88914a1ff13bb576ce77`, `docs/research/PHYSICAL-INFORMATION-SYSTEMS.json`. ## Sources consulted 2026-10-28

- [Library of Congress: using the card catalogue](https://guides.loc.gov/card-catalog/using-the-card-catalog). Author, title and subject offer different access points. Filing is alphabetical with documented exceptions, not simply accession order. Controlled vocabulary can differ from the reader's words.
- [NLM: Card Tricks](https://circulatingnow.nlm.nih.gov/2020/02/06/card-tricks-the-decline-fall-of-a-bibliographic-tool/). The retaining rod keeps cards in place; NLM documents provisional filing above it before senior checking. This describes one institution's practice, not a universal ritual. Its account also punctures nostalgia for the catalogue as a superior search engine.
- [Science Museum: five-line single-cord switchboard, 1971-360](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co33571/five-line-single-cord-switchboard-1900-1909). This specific object connects subscribers through physical plugs and jacks, with a separate operator circuit. It is evidence for embodied routing, not for the capacity or arrangement of every exchange.
- [Science Museum: Information Age gallery guide, The Exchange](https://www.sciencemuseum.org.uk/sites/default/files/2023-11/Large-print-guide-Information-Age.pdf). Operators connected or forwarded calls using cords and sockets. Our persistent contributions and two comparison positions are deliberate digital inventions, not claims about what telephony preserved.

Interface Atlas consulted at `43cbc51e7af985e904338fb3fde6c314a00c2e07`, `catalog/entries.json` (including `motion`, `react-aria`, `dnd-kit`). Native buttons, CSS layout and SVG suffice; a drag library, physics engine or animation dependency would not establish either proposition. No new Atlas technique has been validated for contribution.

## 012: distinction, alternatives, choice

A catalogue is a maintained landscape, not eight isolated search results. A useful physical act is keeping a finger at a known place while scanning what sits nearby. The digital transgression is to keep a record stationary while changing the filing principle around it. The source loses its fixed geography; the reader keeps their orientation.

Rejected as the main move: cabinet-opening spectacle (containers become the task); more query controls (retrieval still dominates); generic pin-and-compare (no reason why these neighbours). Chosen: a working spread with title, subject and shared-vocabulary orders. Unheld browsing preserves a position; holding preserves the record. Every neighbour exposes its placement reason. Previous neighbours remain named when an order changes. Accession IDs identify records, not public alphabetical order. The existing exact-term query remains a separate, inspectable baseline.

## 013: distinction, alternatives, choice

The first diagram had no consequence to connecting everything. Physical cords imply establishment, occupation and clearing. Translate this into two spaces for actual pairwise reading: a proposed relation can be patched, opened for comparison, then released. A third route cannot silently replace occupied work. Sources remain accessible when the desk is full or empty.

Rejected as the main move: more graph filters (attention remains free and invisible); simulated ringing/consent (fictional authors cannot answer); a connection form (already explored downstream and risks approval theatre). Chosen: a limited reading desk. Two is an explicit experimental interface constraint, not a historical capacity or an assertion of how many thoughts humans can hold. Opening a circuit shows the two unchanged contributions and the curator's reason side by side. Release has a visible consequence: it clears space without deleting speech.

## What would falsify the moves?

If holding never changes orientation, 012 is a pin button in costume. If reaching capacity does not change the next available action, or release erases source speech, 013 misses its mechanism. If a visitor needs the prose to notice either consequence, the interaction needs further work. No mechanics test can establish delight, cultural resonance, comprehension or retrieval quality; those remain review questions.
