# Study index

One line of interpretation per study, so a new session can see what the collection has already tried without reading every note. The catalogue facts (number, title, route, technology, status) live in [`src/data/samples.ts`](../../src/data/samples.ts) and are not repeated here; `npm run check:docs` fails if this index and the catalogue list different study numbers. Numbers are not contiguous: 002 and 005 were never published.

For each study: the **central relation** carried from the encounter, the **signature interaction** that carries it, and what from this study **must not become a default** for the next one. The last column is the point of the index.

### 001 — Waschlumpe
- Relation: many weak loops form a resilient surface; local tension propagates through a loosely coupled network, and holes are structural rather than defective.
- Signature: a broad, delayed pointer field introduces soft tension into a runtime-generated knit; explicitly not a click ripple or a magnetic pull.
- Not a default: a Canvas textile hero, or pointer tension as a generic hover effect.

### 003 — Soft Green
- Relation: fine fibres become load-bearing through twist; packed diagonal bands and fibre-scale roughness.
- Signature: a material study. A raw WebGL height field lit as dry wound fibre; pointer input moves only the light.
- Not a default: WebGL or a procedural height field as the way to show any material; the shader is precedent for this cord only.

### 004 — Blue Coil
- Relation: a surface accumulated by wrapping around a centre, with compression, binding marks and the drift of hand construction.
- Signature: a material study. Concentric procedural relief; light direction changes, the material stays still.
- Not a default: stillness, or the pointer-as-light convention shared with 003.

### 006 — Flaked Blue
- Relation: one erosion history orders the layers (intact paint, lifted edge, undercoat, exposed wood); the order is legible through height and shadow, not colour alone.
- Signature: a material study. A layered erosion field with a derived height map and micro-shadow.
- Not a default: stratigraphy as the metaphor for every kind of history.

### 007 — Blue Printed Paper
- Relation: every mark inherits the same substrate: one shared pulp and absorption field gives all marks the same feathering, drift and missing coverage.
- Signature: a material study. Seeded radial print systems over one coherent error field.
- Not a default: "one coherent error field" as a rule for every procedural texture.

### 008 — WordArt
- Relation: expressive display typography existed as a directly manipulable object inside utilitarian office software; visible possibility and exact, cheap control rather than prompting.
- Signature: editable text with preset galleries, warp, fill, outline, extrusion and selection handles; the recipe travels in SVG metadata and the URL hash and can be reopened.
- Not a default: a retro skin, or preset rows and "Now" variants on every tool. Note: two directories (`008-wordart`, `008-word-art`) and two routes exist; the catalogue lists only `008-wordart`.

### 009 — Stitched Care
- Relation: thousands of deliberate decisions on a humble support; one stitch grid describes both motifs and field.
- Signature: the same model drawn at two distances, and a labelled "Stitches completed" control that reveals the work as a count. The finished textile is the default state; nothing autoplays.
- Not a default: a stitched progress bar for every textile, or any ordering of making presented as how the object was made.

### 010 — Pixels to Stitches
- Relation: pixels and stitches share a chart, yet embroidery reads as warmer and handmade; which qualities produce that impression at interface size.
- Signature: a material and perceptual study, named as such. A still five-treatment comparison with switchable qualities, then a small working list with stitched states on native controls.
- Not a default: attaching a story about care to justify the appearance, or treating this as a behavioural transformation.

### 011 — Thread Icons
- Relation: embroidery as a contemporary digital medium; threads follow each shape (satin fills, fanned stitches, cord outlines, knots).
- Signature: a build-time SVG stitch vocabulary producing six icons at 24, 32 and 48 px, shown in a working toolbar with selected, notification and upload states.
- Not a default: the stitch vocabulary as the collection's icon style, or build-time sprites as the way to do icons.

### 012 — The Impossible Index
- Relation: a card catalogue is a prior act of classification made navigable; a reader keeps a place while the filing principle changes around it.
- Signature: hold a record while title, subject and shared-vocabulary orders rearrange its neighbours; every neighbour exposes its placement reason; unmatched originals are retained.
- Not a default: a generic filtered list or pin-and-compare; cabinet-opening spectacle.

### 013 — The Switchboard
- Relation: temporary connections between independent voices; connecting occupies something and releasing clears it without deleting speech.
- Signature: a two-space reading desk where a relation is patched, opened for side-by-side reading, and released; a third cannot silently replace occupied work.
- Not a default: "two" as a claim about attention, connection forms, or simulated ringing and consent.

### 014 — Made Heavy
- Relation: interfaces have shadow without load; brutalism has load that produces its own shadow. What carries what becomes legible, and depth has consequences for what you can do.
- Signature: regions of the study's prose rest on movable piers and fall when the last one is removed; a bay can be acted on only while faced squarely, with depth zero exposing the flat interface as the peer. Two further experiments judge arrangement and pour text in sequence.
- Not a default: the paper-and-ink palette, the four-experiment plural form, the façade, or concrete grey. The study's rules (cantilever limit, exception verdict) are its own, not theory.
