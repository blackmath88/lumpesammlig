# 013 — The Switchboard

## Encounter
The encounter is the manual telephone switchboard: separate endpoints, physical sockets, temporary circuits and an operator who connects lines. The voices belong to their endpoints, not to the switchboard. A circuit can disappear without erasing the conversations or the parties involved.

The study asks what this separation between **source** and **routing** can become in an interactive medium. It is not an ATProto demo or a prototype of a particular social product.

## Situated reading
The operator chooses which lines to connect. The lines retain their own endpoints. A connection can be broken; the original speech still happened. Crucially, the operator is a mediator, not the owner or author of each voice.

## Competing readings
- **Routing:** a conversation emerges by connecting independent contributions.
- **Perspective:** different connections make different context visible without changing source records.
- **Power:** who controls the switchboard? This metaphor risks re-centralizing authority.

## Transformation
A set of attributed, synthetic contributions remains fixed. The visitor changes the *view connections* by selecting a topic, and sees which replies, references and disagreements are related. The contribution text, author and provenance do not mutate. No invented live ATProto posts or fabricated consensus.

## What this tests
Can readers distinguish the underlying records from the temporary connections proposed by a viewing interface? Does a switchboard explain decentralization or mistakenly imply one central operator? Compare later with an overlay metaphor if the latter dominates.

## Provenance and later applications
The telephone switchboard is the operative reference; the experiment follows Lumpesammlig's encounter → interpretation → transformation method. [br-ai-nstorm](https://github.com/blackmath88/br-ai-nstorm), [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com/) and [Postack Underflow](https://github.com/blackmath88/postack-underflow) are possible **downstream applications and conceptual relatives**, not the study's subject or dependency. [Colors](https://github.com/blackmath88/colors) informed the print treatment. The source mechanism, not the destination project, should determine the experiment.

## Initial review questions (from the first prototype)
Test topic switching, authorship, explicit disagreements, keyboard navigation and reader comprehension. A fixture is not federation.

## Development comparison · 2026-10-28 (before implementation)

The current hub-and-spoke diagram turns every voice into a satellite of one central problem, hiding why any two contributions connect. A vintage patchboard would make routing tactile but reinforce operator ownership. A topic feed would retain independent records but flatten relationships into co-occurrence. We choose **temporary pairwise circuits**: topic and relationship lenses propose explicit endpoint-to-endpoint connections; every cable has a reason and can be unplugged. The source ledger remains present when all cables disappear. Inspection exposes authorship, source addresses and unmodified records. Disagreement is a relation with two voices, never a synthesized verdict.

This carries temporary routing and independent endpoints without a central node. The operator can erase their entire view but cannot erase a contribution. Synthetic local source addresses demonstrate the boundary structurally, not actual federation, permissions or moderation.

Practice: development-practice baseline, prototype profile and build guidance. Colors 003 informs annotation hierarchy; 004 supplies hard-edged print and deliberate scale; the agreed paper/ink palette dominates, violet marks connections and rose marks disagreement. A sample-local SVG diagram and native buttons are enough; no graph engine or shared abstraction.

## Reflection · implemented specimen, 2026-10-28

**Revealed.** “Unplug all” is the strongest explanation of the ownership boundary: every connection disappears, yet all six voices remain readable with the same author, address and text. Pairwise cables allow an actual reason to be attached to each connection. A topic/relation combination can have no routes while still showing the complete source ledger.

**Weakened.** The central switchboard operator as a model of community. The original hub concentrated authority even though its prose denied ownership. The revised view has no central endpoint. It is closer to a portable patch set than a telephone exchange; that is a useful revision of the source reading.

**Untranslated.** Real speech is synchronous and ephemeral; these records are persistent. There is no federation, source fetching, author authentication, moderation, consent protocol, persistence, export or proof that independent servers remain available. Synthetic `.example` source addresses are displayed as text, not live links. The diagram itself is a visual overview; labelled native buttons provide inspection and cable control.

**Falsification.** Without cables, colour or socket imagery, the visitor can still select pairwise relations, inspect their reasons, remove them and recover unchanged attributed records. The separation between view authority and source authority survives. This does not prove political decentralization; a view can still frame and suppress attention.

**Carry into Postack.** Model contributions and view relations separately. Give each relation a reason and provenance; keep disagreement explicit, retain source access across lenses, and allow multiple disposable readings over the same records. Do not treat a drawn connection as author endorsement or consensus. Human comprehension remains unvalidated.

## Second development · The limited desk, 2026-10-28

The later brief asked what a diagram of unlimited relations left untranslated. Source verification and rejected alternatives are recorded in [the development research](../../../docs/research/012-013-held-place-live-circuits.md). Original source and relation fixtures remain unchanged; the unrestricted map remains available for comparison.

Two circuit positions hold actual pairwise readings. A proposed relation can be patched (occupying a position), opened (showing the unchanged sources together) and released (freeing capacity). A third patch leaves existing work intact and asks the reader to release a circuit. Original contributions remain readable regardless of capacity. No author is being called or asked to consent; every relation is explicitly the specimen's interpretation.

The trial weakened the idea that cable motion establishes mediation. Occupation has to change the next available action, and opening must reveal more than a status label. The source-to-interaction move is limited working attention, not telephone cosplay. Two is an experimental layout constraint, not historical board capacity or a claim about human cognitive limits. No timers, artificial waiting, ringing, remote source fetching or fabricated author endorsements are used. The question for human review: does making room for a relation give it weight, or merely add friction?
