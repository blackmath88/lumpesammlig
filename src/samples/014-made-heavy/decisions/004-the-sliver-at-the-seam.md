# 004 — The sliver at the seam

Status: accepted, implemented 2026-10-31. Scope: experiment 04 (pours). Retrospective reconstruction from `../sample.md` ("The sliver is the whole experiment. Hiding the covered text entirely would have made this undo with a texture; showing it in full would have made it a diff.") and `pours.ts`.

**Choice.** When a later pour overcasts a struck one, the covered pour keeps its first words visible as a crushed sliver at the seam, permanently, on the surface. There is no history panel.

**Alternatives the record names.** Hide the covered text entirely (the result would be undo with a formwork texture). Show the covered text in full (the result would be a diff or track-changes view, the stated nearest peer).

**Reasons.** The reading is "the wall as record of manufacture" (report 02 §3.2): construction order stays legible in the finished surface without opening anything. Only the middle option keeps the earlier pour present without reopening it.

**Trade-offs.** The sliver is small and could be missed; whether a surface that keeps its seams reads as honesty or clutter is the open question `sample.md` poses and no one has answered.

**Grounding.** Report 02 §3.2 (board grain, seams, tie positions as finished surface); `../readings/01-tectonic-language.md`.

**Revisit if.** Human review finds the sliver illegible at phone width, or finds that visitors read it as a bug rather than a seam.
