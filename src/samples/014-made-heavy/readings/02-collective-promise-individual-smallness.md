# 02 — Collective promise, individual smallness

Status: **held, then dropped as a direction; untranslated**. Basis: `../sample.md` §What weakened ("The research's social reading ... is not in any of the four. The fifth direction that would have carried it, a page that refuses to adapt to the viewer, was dropped because any monument could produce it."); report 01 §2 and §4; report 02 §5.4. Retrospective reconstruction.

## What it foregrounds

Report 01's defining tension: a building can promise a better shared future while making the individual feel very small. Report 02 §5.4 puts the same point as collective life against institutional authority, and §2 as repetition for provision against experiment for representation. The reading takes scale and address as the subject: the building speaks to a collective and over the person.

## What it leaves out

Construction and the legibility of force. On this reading the piers and ribs are means; the subject is who the building is for.

## The direction it produced, and why it was dropped

`sample.md` names one candidate: a page that refuses to adapt to the viewer, meaning a layout that does not reflow, scale or respond, so that the visitor is made small before it. It was dropped because any monument, indeed any large fixed object, could produce that experiment. It fails the second review question (ten unrelated sources could produce it) and the third (nothing specific in this encounter gives permission that a cathedral would not).

Nothing in the repository records whether other directions for this reading were tried. The decision note is `../decisions/003-drop-the-fifth-direction.md`.

## What remains

The reading is still true to the reports and untranslated. `sample.md` says scale is therefore untranslated. A later experiment could return to it if a behaviour specific to Soviet provision and exception, rather than to monumentality in general, could be found. Experiment 03 (fabric versus exception) is the nearest existing point of contact: it carries the provision-exception distinction from report 02 §2 as a rule about arrangement, not as a social claim.
