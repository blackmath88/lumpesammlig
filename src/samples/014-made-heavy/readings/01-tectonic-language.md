# 01 — The tectonic language

Status: **chosen**. Basis: `../sample.md` (Situated reading, Distinction, Transformations), commit `2756f40`. Retrospective reconstruction of why it was chosen; the reading itself is stated in `sample.md` in the study's own words.

## What it foregrounds

Report 02's central claim: the effect does not come from concrete but from making a building appear to bear, suspend, resist, repeat or concentrate force. The reading treats that sentence as a description of behaviour and takes the feature table (§4) as a vocabulary of distinct behaviours: bearing (piers, raised volume, cantilever), screening (deep façade, ribs), arrangement (modules, massing) and trace (cast surfaces).

The distinction from the nearest interface peer is the one `sample.md` states: elevation gives interfaces shadow without load; brutalism has load that produces its own shadow. What carries what is legible; depth has consequences.

The caution in report 02 §4.3, that the image of support and the engineering need not agree, is part of this reading rather than a separate one. It is what makes experiment 01 report a monumental pier that carries nothing as rhetorical.

## What it leaves out

Everything social and historical: who commissioned, who was dignified or made small, what the buildings meant and mean. It also leaves out material (concrete, stone facing, mosaics) by design, which is why the page is paper and ink.

## Why four experiments rather than one

Because the four behaviours in the vocabulary are different enough that a composite would test none of them. `sample.md` says this directly ("The study is deliberately plural"). See `../decisions/001-four-separate-experiments.md`.

## What would weaken it

- If the Vassiliev attribution in report 02 §4.3 does not hold, the rhetorical pier loses its stated grounding (see `../research/sources.md`).
- `sample.md` already records that the rhetorical pier is hard to feel without the load-path toggle, and that formwork grain in experiment 04 is decorative by the repository's own test. Both are admitted weaknesses of this reading's execution, not of the reading.
