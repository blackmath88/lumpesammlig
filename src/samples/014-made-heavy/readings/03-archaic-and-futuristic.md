# 03 — The archaic and the futuristic

Status: **noted as untranslated; never developed**. Basis: `../sample.md` §What weakened ("the archaic-plus-futuristic double image, which belongs to material and silhouette rather than to behaviour"); report 01 §3 ("a future imagined through mass rather than lightness"); report 02 §5.2. Retrospective reconstruction.

## What it foregrounds

The double image both reports describe: buildings that resemble machines, spacecraft or imagined future cities and at the same time read as geological or ancient mass. Report 01 attaches it to the Soviet fascination with space and science; report 02 §5.2 treats it as an aesthetic concept arising from the material.

## What it leaves out

Structure, support and arrangement. This reading is about silhouette, surface and association.

## Why it was not developed

`sample.md` places it in material and silhouette rather than behaviour. By the repository's own test that makes it a candidate for a material or perceptual study, not for a behavioural transformation, and the study chose the behavioural route throughout. There is no evidence that a material study was proposed and rejected; the reading was simply set aside.

## What remains

A legitimate separate study could take it up as a named material study, in the way 003, 004, 006 and 007 are material studies: which observable qualities make a surface read as both archaic and futuristic at interface size. That would need visual evidence this encounter does not have.
