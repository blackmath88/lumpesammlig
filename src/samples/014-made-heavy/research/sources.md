## Limitations

- No primary architectural sources (drawings, project documentation, conservation studies, Vassiliev's own text) have been read.
- The reports are an assistant's synthesis; their paragraphs are not quotations and their tables are their own constructions.
- The study's experiments do not depend on any building-specific fact being true. They depend on the general vocabulary (bearing, screening, arrangement, trace) and on the caution about the image of support. If the Vassiliev attribution turned out to be wrong, experiment 01's "rhetorical pier" would need a different grounding or an honest note.
- No Interface Atlas lookup was recorded for this study; the implementation used plain HTML, CSS and TypeScript and the commit does not mention outside technique. Whether an Atlas entry would have helped is unknown.
