# Encounter evidence · 014

**Status: researched, not visited.** There is no photograph, site visit, drawing or first-hand conversation in this encounter. The two files here are secondary research supplied by the author on 2026-10-31. They were produced with a web-research assistant (Perplexity) and cite their own sources inline; a logo image line at the top of each export was removed, nothing else was edited.

- `research-01-conceptualisation.md` — a short interpretive framing: "utopia made heavy", the Russian/Soviet distinction, structure as ornament, collective monumentality, futurism and weight, present-day melancholy.
- `research-02-materials-and-tectonics.md` — a longer account of construction and aesthetic logic: cast-in-place versus precast, the feature table (piers, raised volume, cantilever, deep façade, repeated modules, massing, sun-screen ribs), image of support versus actual engineering, and six questions to bring to a building.

What the study treats as evidence is therefore a *description of a tectonic language*, not an object. Every claim about a specific building in the experiments is taken from these files and should be checked against primary sources before being relied on. Where the research itself says it cannot establish something (for example the façade assembly of the Robotics and Cybernetics Centre), the study does not pretend otherwise.

The conversation in which the directions were chosen is summarised in `../sample.md`.
